Last updated: 22 October 2026.

This policy applies to paid SharePilot licences. Purchases are not available yet; the current download is a trial preview.

## Try SharePilot before buying

SharePilot includes a seven-day trial. Use it to check that the app works with your Mac, network and file server before purchasing a licence.

## Refunds in exceptional circumstances

Purchases are normally final. Beyond refunds required by law or available under Paddle’s terms, I consider discretionary refunds only in exceptional circumstances, case by case. An exception is not guaranteed and does not create a general money-back guarantee.

A change of mind or no longer needing the app does not normally qualify for a discretionary refund from me. This does not limit any cancellation or refund right you otherwise have.

## Your rights and Paddle

Paddle processes purchases as merchant of record. Its [buyer terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/buyer-terms) and [refund policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy) also apply. Paddle may independently approve a discretionary refund for a request submitted within 14 days of purchase.

Nothing in this policy limits mandatory consumer rights, including applicable withdrawal rights and remedies for faulty, misdescribed or undelivered software. These rights do not depend on exceptional circumstances. See Paddle’s refund policy for the withdrawal periods and digital-content exceptions that apply in your country.

## Requesting help or a refund

For a problem with SharePilot or an exceptional refund request, [contact me](/contact.html?subject=SharePilot%20refund#my-form) with your Paddle order reference. Explain what happened if you are asking me to consider an exception.

You can also request a refund or exercise withdrawal rights directly through [Paddle’s buyer support](https://paddle.net), using the support link in your receipt. You do not need my approval to contact Paddle or exercise statutory rights.

An approved refund revokes the licence associated with that purchase.
